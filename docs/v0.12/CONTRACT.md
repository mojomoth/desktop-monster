User-approved plan (2026-09-21, `~/.claude-mojomoth-account/plans/v0-12-0-jolly-map.md`). Baseline: annotated tag `v0.11.0` at 86b1d97 plus the committed HUD follow-up; version 0.12.0. Legacy Ralph `CURRENT=v3`, v10 and v11 stay unchanged.

## Behavior
- Boss raid = server-authoritative cycle (`RAID_EPOCH`, `periodMs`) with lazy phases gathering → countdown → confirming → battle → settled / skipped; five bosses, one per element, fixed rotation. Conditions are collective (`conditionNeed` distinct qualifying players per condition); unlockers get `priorityMs` before open entry up to `capacity`; countdown 12–48 h.
- No native notifications or dialogs for game interactions: confirmation is the in-game pixel `참여` button at the top centre (or the same pixel popup inside the menu window); `dialog.showMessageBox` (3 sites) and the theft `Notification` are replaced by `src/menu/popup.ts`; only the OS save dialog remains.
- Battle: heroes only, every sprite at scale 1 on the unchanged 200×130 canvas, boss ≤128×88 art pixels, participants seeded left/right, my hero marked (arrow + `LV n`, no XP bar), my damage floats over my head, red boss HP gauge and blue time gauge. Field monster and XP untouched during a raid; PvP replay queue preserved.
- Real time = 1 s polling with batched `POST /v1/raid/attack` (server clamps to `attack_i × clicks × crit/fever × slack`); raid routes bypass the players table lock with their own 150/min budget; hot state in module memory flushed every `flushMs` and on phase transitions.
- Boss HP fixed at battle entry from confirmed players: `Σ attack_i × cadencePerSec × battleMs/1000 × clearRatioBps/10000`. Rewards on kill: `xpLevels × xpToNext(level)`, `goldKills × coinsForIndex(bestIndex)`, one raid item by rank odds `max(floor, top × decay^(k−1))`; timeout pays `failRewardBps`; contribution below `contributionFloorBps` pays the fail tier. Claims are idempotent server-side and applied exactly once through the coordinator boundary + RecoveryStore.
- Raid items reuse the epic rarity with `raidBossId` provenance in a separate `RAID_CATALOG` (the 224-item `EQUIPMENT_CATALOG` pin is unchanged); 10 Codex-drawn icons. Menu gets a ninth `레이드` tab on one 560 px row.
- All tunables are plain numeric literals in `RAID_PARAMETERS` (`src/core/raid.ts`); the balance harness substitutes candidates into isolated compiled copies and the independent verifier parses the AST.
